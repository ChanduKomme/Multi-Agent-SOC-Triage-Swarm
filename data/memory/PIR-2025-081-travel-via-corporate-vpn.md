# PIR-2025-081: Impossible travel caused by the Lisbon VPN egress
verdict: benign | severity: low | techniques: T1078

An engineer appeared to sign in from Berlin and Lisbon within an hour. The second sign-in came from the corporate VPN
egress in Lisbon and the engineer has Portugal listed as a home country.

Resolution: closed. Lesson: check whether the far-away address belongs to corporate egress before escalating travel.
